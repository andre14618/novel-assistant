# Story Ask

Genre lane: mercenary-progression adventure (L109). Novel: Rillgate.

Kael Rusk, an unranked Rillgate laborer with an iron-thread-scarred past,
has four days before his sister Mira's debt marker is sold outside guild
protection — after which a Deep Markets creditor owns her body. The only
contract that pays before the bell is the Gray Salt Mine deep-salvage run,
which has killed three men in six months and requires iron-thread binding.
The law only protects a claim through admissible evidence or bronze rank;
Kael has neither.

Chapter-1 arc (MPA-01/02/03 hub pressure + contract offer + crew friction):
Kael learns the deadline, tests the creditor, and accepts Orin Vale's
warning that only admissible evidence or bronze rank can protect Mira. He
leaves the Contract Hall with broker Lady Varn's address as his only lead
and the contract unsigned.
