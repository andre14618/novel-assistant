# Rillgate — style (genre/tone)

Genre lane: mercenary-progression adventure (L109).

Match the voice properties of the lane — register, cadence, and sentence
construction — not any specific source text or primer.

## Register

- Hard, transactional, survival-first. The POV character measures the world
  in contracts, ranks, and what things cost.
- Competence over sentiment: skill, leverage, and price are the currency;
  feeling is stated plainly, never melodramatic.
- Rank and debt are concrete: a rank is a threshold to cross, a debt is a
  countdown against a bell.

## Cadence

- Short declarative sentences for action, pressure, and decision; longer
  sentences only to carry a consequence or a calculation.
- Punch lines on the beat of a threat or a payment; no throat-clearing.
- Dialogue is lean: short tags, physical reaction over adverbs.

## Voice properties

- Grounded physicality: cold, metal, weight, the effort of moving through a
  place that is trying to kill you.
- Sensory restraint: one or two concrete details per paragraph, not overload.
- Stakes are external and timed — a bell, a deadline, a body count — not
  internal monologue.
- No genre-flavor filler; the world earns its strangeness through cost.
