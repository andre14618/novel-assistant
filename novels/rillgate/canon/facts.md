# Facts

- [world] [id=fact-1] Guild contract law protects debt claims through admissible evidence (witness marks, toll logs, sealed marks) or rank standing; raw courage alone is not enough.
- [world] [id=fact-2] A debt marker sold outside guild protection leaves the debtor's protection by the sale date; the bell marks the deadline.
- [world] [id=fact-3] Bronze rank is required to accept the contracts that pay before the tenth; unranked laborers cannot take them.
- [world] [id=fact-4] The Gray Salt Mine deep-salvage run requires iron-thread binding and has killed three men in the last six months; it is the only posting that pays before the tenth.
- [world] [id=fact-5] Paper is a weapon in Rillgate: seals, witness marks, toll logs, rank tokens, and debt markers change what a character can claim or safely do.
- [character] [id=fact-6] Kael Rusk is unranked; his sister Mira Rusk's marker goes to sale in four days.
- [place] [id=fact-7] The Rillgate Contract Hall posts contracts by rank; the debt-market district holds the creditor stalls.
- [place] [id=fact-8] Lady Varn is a broker whose address is Kael's only lead outside guild protection.
